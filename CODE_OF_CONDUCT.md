# Code of conduct

This is a small research project, and the people most likely to take part are
historians, students and archivists checking figures against a book. The
expectation is ordinary scholarly courtesy.

- Disagree with the evidence, not the person. A report that a figure is wrong is
  welcome however it is phrased; it is the most useful thing anyone can send.
- Say where a claim comes from. For this project that usually means a table
  number and a cell, or a page of the printed compendium.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning remarks about anyone's
  background, nationality, language or level of experience. The source concerns
  a colonial period and questions of identity run through it; discuss them as
  history.

Conduct that breaks this can be reported privately to the maintainer through
their GitHub profile, [@peterlo920307](https://github.com/peterlo920307). Reports
are read by the maintainer only. Comments, commits or issues that break it may be
edited or removed, and repeated breaches will lead to being blocked from the
repository.

This is written for this project, and follows the intent of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
